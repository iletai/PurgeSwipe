Storage full? Clean your photo library in minutes with a swipe.

PurgeSwipe turns cleanup into a quick card game. Pick a deck, swipe left to trash, swipe right to keep. When you are done, confirm one system deletion and see how much space you freed.

DECKS
• Screenshots: every screenshot in one stack
• Similar shots: near-duplicates grouped, the best one auto-kept
• Blurry photos: out-of-focus shots found for you
• Large videos: the biggest space hogs first (Pro)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is analyzed on your iPhone
• No account, no cloud upload, no analytics
• Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, so you can still recover them

FREE AND PRO
Free: unlimited swiping, uncapped first cleanup, then up to 25 deletions a day, with banner ads.
Pro: unlimited deletions, all similar and blurry groups, large videos deck, no ads.

PurgeSwipe Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription (the yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.
